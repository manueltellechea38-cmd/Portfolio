05 — The Gossip Section

The Gossip Section

A news story narrated in audio format.

Darwin Núñez — Tienda Inglesa

The Story (English)

Hey everyone, welcome back with this week's gossip. According to sources close to the player — basically my cousin who saw something on Twitter — Darwin Núñez was visiting Montevideo recently and walked into a Tienda Inglesa supermarket in the city center to buy yerba mate and biscochos. Totally normal, right? The thing is, absolute chaos broke out because everyone recognized him and nobody would let him get to the checkout. People were asking for photos, kids were asking for autographs on their croissants, and the security guard had to help him get out. Darwin left without his pastries, but happy. The most Uruguayan thing ever: the best striker in the world, stuck in a supermarket line because of mate.

Why I chose this

I chose memes as my creative activity because they are a modern form of communication used every day on social media. They combine humor with real situations that everyone can relate to, especially about work and daily life. I think memes are a fun and creative way to express ideas in English.

A Special Thank You
to My English Teacher

Dear Dahiana,

Even though I only met you this year, Dahiana, you are a teacher I will always remember. This is my last year at school, and I'm happy I had the opportunity to have you as my teacher.

You are a very talkative person, always making jokes and full of energy. It's impossible to forget your rubber pig, the candies you were always asking for, or how you changed your water bottle all the time, until one day you came with a Los Andes bottle. And something funny is that you are from Uruguay, but you don't drink mate!

I also liked learning a little about your life, like when you told us that you worked as a teacher in the United States, how much you like rustic things, and your plans to travel soon.

Even though we only had one year together, I have very good memories of your classes and your personality. Now that I'm graduating, I want to thank you for your time, your patience, and for making English classes fun.

Thank you so much for this year, Dahiana! I hope you enjoy your next trip, and I hope you never run out of candies or lose your rubber pig!

With thanks,
Manuel
