05 — The Gossip Section

The Gossip Section

A news story narrated in audio format.

Darwin Núñez — Tienda Inglesa

The Story (English)

Hey everyone, welcome back with this week's gossip. According to sources close to the player — basically my cousin who saw something on Twitter — Darwin Núñez was visiting Montevideo recently and walked into a Tienda Inglesa supermarket in the city center to buy yerba mate and biscochos. Totally normal, right? The thing is, absolute chaos broke out because everyone recognized him and nobody would let him get to the checkout. People were asking for photos, kids were asking for autographs on their croissants, and the security guard had to help him get out. Darwin left without his pastries, but happy. The most Uruguayan thing ever: the best striker in the world, stuck in a supermarket line because of mate.

Why I chose this

I chose memes as my creative activity because they are a modern form of communication used every day on social media. They combine humor with real situations that everyone can relate to, especially about work and daily life. I think memes are a fun and creative way to express ideas in English.
